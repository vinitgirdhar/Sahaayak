FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out once you're signed in — the AI assistant can help too.

Is Sahaayak free for street vendors?

Yes. Creating a vendor account, browsing wholesalers, and placing orders is completely free. You only pay for the goods you order.

How are wholesalers verified?

Every wholesaler application is reviewed and approved by the Sahaayak admin team before their products appear in the marketplace. Ongoing vendor reviews then feed each wholesaler's public trust score.

Can I order from multiple wholesalers at once?

Yes — that's the point. Add products from any number of suppliers to one cart and check out in a single step. Each wholesaler fulfils their part of your order.

How do payments work?

Pay the way the market works — UPI, card, or cash on delivery. You can securely save your preferred payment methods to speed up checkout.

Does Sahaayak work on my phone?

Yes. The entire experience — browsing, cart, checkout, order management — is designed mobile-first, so it works beautifully on any smartphone browser. No app install needed.
